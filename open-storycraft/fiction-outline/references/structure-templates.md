# Story Structure Templates

Four proven structures for outlining fiction. Choose based on story needs.

---

## 3-Act Structure (8 Beats)

Classic and flexible. Works for most stories.

| Beat | Name | Purpose | Anti-Flat Note |
|------|------|---------|----------------|
| 1 | **Setup** | Ordinary world; establish what protagonist values | Plant what will be sacrificed |
| 2 | **Inciting Incident** | Disruption forces action | Goal is WRONG or incomplete |
| 3 | **First Act Turn** | Point of no return | Protagonist commits to flawed goal |
| 4 | **Rising Action** | Tests, allies, enemies | Power must shift; ally has agenda |
| 5 | **Midpoint** | False victory OR revelation | Goal shifts; truth cracks |
| 6 | **Crisis** | Antagonist gains upper hand | Protagonist loses something unrecoverable |
| 7 | **Climax** | Final confrontation | Cost paid; real goal achieved |
| 8 | **Resolution** | New equilibrium | Show transformation |

---

## Hero's Journey (12 Beats)

Mythic structure. Best for transformation arcs and epic scope.

| Beat | Name | Purpose | Anti-Flat Note |
|------|------|---------|----------------|
| 1 | **Ordinary World** | Establish normal life | Plant what will be sacrificed |
| 2 | **Call to Adventure** | Disruption arrives | Initial goal is incomplete |
| 3 | **Refusal of the Call** | Hesitation | False belief keeps them stuck |
| 4 | **Meeting the Mentor** | Wisdom offered | This wisdom will be tested/subverted |
| 5 | **Crossing the Threshold** | Commitment | Point of no return |
| 6 | **Tests, Allies, Enemies** | New world challenges | Introduce conflicted ally |
| 7 | **Approach to Inmost Cave** | Preparing for ordeal | Stakes clarify |
| 8 | **Ordeal (Midpoint)** | Death and rebirth | False truth cracks; goal shifts |
| 9 | **Reward** | Seize the sword | Hidden cost attached |
| 10 | **The Road Back** | Antagonist strikes | Power shifts to antagonist |
| 11 | **Resurrection (Climax)** | Final battle | Pay the real cost |
| 12 | **Return with Elixir** | Transformed return | New equilibrium; show change |

---

## 5-Act Structure (Shakespearean)

Dramatic and literary. Best for tragedy and complex drama.

| Act | Name | Purpose | Anti-Flat Note |
|-----|------|---------|----------------|
| 1 | **Exposition** | World and stakes | Establish what will be lost |
| 2 | **Rising Action** | Complications mount | Allies and enemies; agenda conflicts |
| 3 | **Climax (Midpoint)** | Maximum tension; major turn | Everything changes here |
| 4 | **Falling Action** | Consequences unfold | Power shifts; costs accumulate |
| 5 | **Denouement** | Resolution | Cost paid; new equilibrium |

**Note**: In tragedy, Act 3 is the protagonist's highest point before the fall. In comedy, Act 3 is the darkest before resolution.

## Save the Cat (15 Beats)

Precise and commercial. Best for tight pacing and clear emotional beats.

| Beat | Name | Purpose | Anti-Flat Note |
|------|------|---------|----------------|
| 1 | **Opening Image** | Visual "before" | Shows who protagonist WAS |
| 2 | **Theme Stated** | Someone says the theme | Protagonist ignores/rejects it |
| 3 | **Setup** | Establish world and status quo | Plant what will be lost |
| 4 | **Catalyst** | Disruption | Forces change |
| 5 | **Debate** | Should they act? | False belief holds them back |
| 6 | **Break into Two** | Commit to action | Threshold crossed; no return |
| 7 | **B Story** | Subplot begins | Will collide with A story |
| 8 | **Fun and Games** | Promise of premise | Plant problems amid victories |
| 9 | **Midpoint** | False victory or defeat | Stakes raise; goal shifts |
| 10 | **Bad Guys Close In** | Antagonist gains ground | Allies waver; trust fractures |
| 11 | **All Is Lost** | Lowest point | False belief shattered |
| 12 | **Dark Night of the Soul** | Despair and choice | Choose who to become |
| 13 | **Break into Three** | Armed with truth | Commit to REAL goal |
| 14 | **Finale** | Climax | Cost paid; transformation complete |
| 15 | **Final Image** | Visual "after" | Mirrors opening; shows change |

---

## Choosing a Structure

| Story Type | Recommended Structure |
|------------|----------------------|
| Action/Adventure | 3-Act or Hero's Journey |
| Romance | Save the Cat |
| Thriller | 3-Act (tight pacing) |
| Epic Fantasy | Hero's Journey |
| Literary Drama | 5-Act |
| Coming-of-Age | Hero's Journey or Save the Cat |
| Tragedy | 5-Act |
| Mystery | 3-Act with revelation at Midpoint |

**Remember**: Structures are scaffolding, not cages. Adapt as needed.
