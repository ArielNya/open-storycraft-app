# Continuity Tracking

Scene documents maintain story continuity so the writer doesn't have to remember everything.

---

## What to Track

### Timeline
- **Day number**: "Day 34" or "Week 3, Tuesday"
- **Time of day**: Morning, noon, dusk, midnight, 3am
- **Time since key event**: "Two days after the attack"
- **Deadlines**: "Three days until the summit"

### Physical States
- **Injuries**: Who's hurt, how badly, healing status
- **Exhaustion**: Who hasn't slept, who's running on fumes
- **Appearance changes**: Haircuts, new scars, weight loss
- **Clothing**: What they're wearing, what's been damaged
- **Equipment**: What they have, what they've lost

### Emotional States
- **Carrying forward**: Grief from Chapter 3 doesn't vanish in Chapter 4
- **Building tension**: Unresolved anger accumulates
- **Relationship temperature**: Are they speaking? Avoiding each other?
- **Mood trajectory**: Are things getting better or worse emotionally?

### Location Details
- **Where they are**: Specific place name
- **How they got there**: If arrival wasn't shown
- **What's nearby**: Resources, threats, exits
- **Condition**: Is the place damaged? Changed since last visit?

### Story Threads
- **Open questions**: What the reader is wondering
- **Pending reveals**: Secrets not yet exposed
- **Promises to keep**: Setups awaiting payoff
- **Ticking clocks**: Deadlines approaching

---

## Continuity Notes Format

At the top of each scene document:

```markdown
## Continuity Notes
- Day 34-35 (Night)
- Ch 38: Feral signs returned - dead rabbit, claw marks
- Preston hasn't slept in 48 hours
- Beth's iron poker (acquired Ch 41)
- Unresolved: What happened to the missing livestock?
- Building: Tension between Beth and Thunder over patrol duties
```

Keep it scannable. The writer needs quick reference, not a novel.

## Cross-Chapter Connections

### Callback Tracking
Note what earlier events this chapter references:
- "References Ch 12 conversation about trust"
- "Payoff for Ch 5 foreshadowing (the locked door)"
- "Character remembers promise made in Ch 3"

### Setup Tracking
Note what this chapter sets up for later:
- "Plants seed for Ch 28 betrayal"
- "Establishes skill character will need in climax"
- "Creates expectation that will be subverted"

## Character State Tracking

For each POV chapter, note where the character IS emotionally:

| Character | Enters Chapter | Exits Chapter | Key Shift |
|-----------|---------------|---------------|-----------|
| Beth | Anxious, determined | Exhausted but proud | Taking command worked |
| Preston | Wary, protective | Respectful, aligned | Working as equals |

Track across multiple chapters to ensure arcs progress:

| Chapter | Beth's State |
|---------|-------------|
| 40 | Overwhelmed, doubting herself |
| 41 | Forcing herself to act despite fear |
| 42 | Small victory, growing confidence |
| 43 | Setback, but doesn't break |
| 44 | In command, still scared but functional |

## Relationship Temperature

Track relationship dynamics between key pairs:

```markdown
## Relationship: Beth ↔ Preston

Ch 35: Antagonistic - he resents her authority
Ch 38: Forced cooperation - common threat
Ch 41: Grudging respect - she earned it
Ch 44: Working as equals - shared vigil
Ch 47: Trust established - he follows her lead
```

This prevents relationships from jumping states without development.

---

## Physical Environment Continuity

When returning to a location, note what's changed:

```markdown
## Location: The Barn

Ch 20: Intact, organized, animals calm
Ch 32: Minor damage from storm, temporary repairs
Ch 44: Fortified, animals nervous, newborns in back stall
```

---

## Wardrobe and Props

Characters can't use items they don't have:

```markdown
## Beth's Inventory

- Iron fireplace poker (acquired Ch 41, always carries now)
- Work boots (has since Ch 1)
- Camisole and shorts (sleep clothes, Ch 44)
- Flashlight (batteries dying, noted Ch 42)
```

## Common Continuity Errors

### Timeline Violations
- Character sleeps for "8 hours" but story only progresses 2 hours
- Events happen in wrong order
- Deadline passes without acknowledgment

### Physical Impossibilities
- Injured arm used freely next chapter
- Character wears jacket they lost
- Item appears without being acquired

### Emotional Discontinuity
- Character devastated, then cheerful without transition
- Grudge forgotten without resolution
- Fear disappears without reason

### Location Teleportation
- Character at location without traveling there
- Distance covered too quickly
- Setting changes without explanation

---

## The Continuity Checklist

Before finalizing a scene document:

- [ ] Timeline consistent with previous chapter
- [ ] Character physical states accurate
- [ ] Character emotional states carry forward
- [ ] Location accessible from previous position
- [ ] Required items in character possession
- [ ] Open threads acknowledged
- [ ] Relationships at correct temperature
