# Common Chapter Issues

Frequent problems and how to fix them.

---

## Voice Problems

### Missing Internal Monologue
**Issue**: Narration is external observation without character perspective.
**Fix**: Add character's reaction/opinion to key moments.
**Example**:
- Before: "The room was large and well-furnished."
- After: "The room screamed money—the kind that wanted you to notice. She noticed."

### Passive Protagonist
**Issue**: Character observes and reacts but doesn't drive action.
**Fix**: Give them a decision point. Even small choices show agency.
**Example**:
- Before: "The argument continued around her."
- After: "She let them argue. Every word was information."

### Voice Drift
**Issue**: Character voice changes mid-chapter without reason.
**Fix**: Check characters file. Restore consistent patterns.

---

## Pacing Problems

### Draggy Middle
**Issue**: Chapter sags between opening and ending.
**Fix**: Cut redundant beats. Add tension or conflict.

### Rushed Climax
**Issue**: Key scene resolves too quickly.
**Fix**: Slow down. Add physical sensation. Let moments land.

### No Hook Ending
**Issue**: Chapter stops rather than ends.
**Fix**: End on question, decision, or shift. Create pull forward.
**Example**:
- Before: "She went home and went to bed."
- After: "She went home. The light in her apartment was already on."

---

## Dialogue Problems

### Same Voice Syndrome
**Issue**: All characters speak identically.
**Fix**: Check characters file. Differentiate vocabulary, rhythm, topics.

### Too Much "Said"
**Issue**: Every line tagged with "said."
**Fix**: Replace some tags with action beats.
**Example**:
- Before: "'I don't know,' she said."
- After: "She shrugged. 'I don't know.'"

### No Subtext
**Issue**: Characters say exactly what they mean.
**Fix**: Add gap between what's said and what's meant.

---

## Continuity Errors

### Timeline Confusion
**Issue**: Time passage unclear or contradictory.
**Fix**: Add brief orienting line at scene start.

### Forgotten Details
**Issue**: Injuries, conditions, or facts from earlier ignored.
**Fix**: Reference previous chapter. Check active-context.md.

### Character Knowledge Bleed
**Issue**: Character knows something they shouldn't.
**Fix**: Track information carefully. Who learned what when?

---

## Atmosphere Problems

### Void Setting
**Issue**: Scene happens nowhere—no sense of place.
**Fix**: Add one line of setting in first paragraph.

### Static Environment
**Issue**: Setting established then forgotten.
**Fix**: Weave setting details through action.

### Missing Senses
**Issue**: Only visual description.
**Fix**: Add sound, physical sensation, or smell.

---

## Style Violations

### AI-ism Phrases
**Issue**: "Couldn't help but," "found herself," "a mix of"
**Fix**: Search and replace. Rephrase directly.
**Example**:
- Before: "She couldn't help but notice his hands."
- After: "His hands. She noticed."

### Em Dash Abuse
**Issue**: Em dashes in narrative prose.
**Fix**: Replace with periods, commas, or colons.

### Rule of Threes
**Issue**: "Cold, dark, and empty" patterns.
**Fix**: Use two items or rephrase entirely.

### Purple Prose Words
**Issue**: Cacophony, symphony, tapestry, verdant, etc.
**Fix**: Use simpler, more specific words.

---

## Structure Problems

### Repetitive Sentences
**Issue**: "She did X. She did Y. She did Z."
**Fix**: Vary sentence structure and openings.

### Missing Transitions
**Issue**: Scene jumps feel jarring.
**Fix**: Add brief orienting line or use white space break.

### Overstuffed Scenes
**Issue**: Too much happening at once.
**Fix**: Split into multiple scenes or cut secondary action.

---

## Quick Checklist

Before submitting edits:
- [ ] Forbidden phrases removed
- [ ] Voice consistent throughout
- [ ] Hook ending present
- [ ] Setting grounded
- [ ] Continuity verified
- [ ] Character voices distinct
- [ ] Pacing appropriate
