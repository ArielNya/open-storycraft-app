# Review Criteria - Detailed

Expanded guidance for each review criterion.

---

## 1. POV Voice & Agency

### What to Check
- Is the POV character's voice present throughout?
- Does their internal monologue drive the narrative?
- Do emotions, opinions, and goals inform decisions?
- Are they ACTIVE (making choices) or PASSIVE (things happen to them)?
- Does voice match style guide specifications?

### Red Flags
- Generic narration that could be anyone
- Character observing but not reacting
- Decisions made for them by circumstances
- Internal thought missing in key moments
- Voice shifts inconsistently

### Example Fix
**Weak**: "The door opened and a man entered."
**Strong**: "The door opened. She tracked the man's hands first—habit—then his face. Too confident for a messenger."

---

## 2. Plot Advancement

### What to Check
- What concrete story progress happens?
- Does chapter have clear purpose beyond filling space?
- Is there hook ending pulling readers forward?
- Plot threads: introduced, advanced, or resolved?
- Does chapter fulfill outline purpose?

### Red Flags
- Characters discuss but don't act
- No new information or change
- Chapter ends without momentum
- Purpose unclear even after reading
- Retreads ground from earlier chapters

### Example Fix
**Weak ending**: "She went to bed, tired from the day's events."
**Strong ending**: "She went to bed. The letter stayed on the table, unopened. It could wait until morning. It couldn't be that important."

---

## 3. Continuity Check

### What to Check
- Does chapter flow from previous one?
- Character positions, time, locations consistent?
- Do characters remember recent events?
- Any contradictions with established facts?
- Injuries, conditions, states tracked?

### Red Flags
- Time jump confusion
- Characters knowing things they shouldn't
- Forgotten injuries or conditions
- Location details contradicting world.md
- Emotional state not matching last chapter's end

### How to Fix
- Add brief orienting line if time passed
- Check characters file for details
- Verify against previous chapter's ending
- Flag contradictions for global edit

---

## 4. Character Authenticity

### What to Check
- Do characters speak in established voices?
- Is there growth, revelation, or progression?
- Actions consistent with personality?
- Power dynamics accurate to arc position?
- Relationships ring true?

### Red Flags
- All characters sound the same
- Personality shifts without cause
- Out-of-character decisions
- Relationship dynamics flat
- No growth or change

### How to Check
- Cross-reference characters.csv for dialogue style
- Compare to earlier appearances
- Verify relationship dynamics match established pattern

---

## 5. Reader Appeal & Genre Fit

### What to Check
- Does chapter meet genre expectations?
- Will readers feel satisfied or frustrated?
- Pacing right for this story point?
- Tone consistent with style?
- Delivers what genre promises?

### Red Flags
- Romance with no romantic tension
- Thriller with no suspense
- Horror that's not unsettling
- Pacing drags or rushes inappropriately
- Tone shifts jarringly

### Genre Checkpoints
- **Romance**: Chemistry present? Tension building?
- **Thriller**: Stakes clear? Suspense maintained?
- **Fantasy**: Magic/world integrated naturally?
- **Horror**: Dread building? Restraint used?

---

## 6. Plot Logic

### What to Check
- Logic holes or contrived situations?
- Character decisions make sense?
- Too convenient or coincidental?
- Unearned emotional beats?
- Setup for payoffs present?

### Red Flags
- "Lucky" timing without setup
- Characters acting stupid to serve plot
- Emotional reactions out of proportion
- Missing motivation for actions
- Deus ex machina saves

### How to Fix
- Add setup earlier for convenient events
- Give characters reasonable motivation
- Earn emotional beats with buildup
- Make obstacles genuine

---

## 7. Sensory Atmosphere

### What to Check
- Established atmosphere present?
- Physical sensations for scene?
- Environment affects behavior?
- Setting details woven naturally?
- At least 2-3 senses touched?

### Red Flags
- Scene happens in void (no setting)
- All description visual only
- Atmosphere mentioned once then forgotten
- Senses not appropriate to moment
- Purple prose (over-description)

### Quick Fix
Add one line of sensory grounding:
- What they hear (or the silence)
- Temperature or air quality
- Physical sensation of emotion

---

## 8. World-Building Accuracy

### What to Check
- Locations correct per world document?
- Technology/magic consistent with rules?
- Cultural customs followed?
- Setting authentic to established world?
- No anachronisms or contradictions?

### Red Flags
- Location details wrong
- Magic/tech breaks established rules
- Characters ignore cultural norms without reason
- Setting feels generic, not specific
- Terminology inconsistent

### How to Check
- Cross-reference world.md
- Verify terminology table
- Check location profiles

---

## 9. Style Guide Compliance

### What to Check
- Forbidden phrases absent?
- Sentence structure varies?
- Dialogue formatted correctly?
- Style rules followed?
- POV handled correctly?

### Common Violations
- "Couldn't help but"
- "Found herself"
- "A mix of X and Y"
- Em dashes in narrative
- Triadic lists
- Wrong dialogue tags

### How to Fix
- Search for forbidden phrases
- Vary sentence openings
- Replace fancy dialogue tags with "said"
- Remove narrative em dashes
