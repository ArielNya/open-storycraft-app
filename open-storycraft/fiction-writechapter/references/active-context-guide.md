# Active Context Guide

How to maintain the tracking file throughout writing.

---

## Purpose

The active-context.md file:
- Tracks progress through the story
- Records what each chapter establishes
- Catches continuity issues before they compound
- Holds decisions for later revision

---

## When to Update

Update after EVERY chapter:

1. **Mark chapter complete** with word count
2. **Set next chapter** target
3. **Log key achievement** (what this chapter established)
4. **Note new continuity** details (names, locations, relationships)
5. **Flag inconsistencies** for later fixing

---

## Timeline Section

Include for stories where tracking time matters:

- Mysteries (clue timing, alibis)
- Romances with schedules
- Pregnancies or growth arcs
- Seasonal events
- Deadlines or countdowns

**Format:**
```markdown
### [Time Period]
| Day/Date | Chapters | Key Events |
|----------|----------|------------|
| Day 1 | 1-2 | [Events] |
```

Omit for stories where time is fluid.

---

## Pending Details Section

Track decisions deferred for later:

- Worldbuilding not yet pinned down
- Character backstory to reveal
- Rules not yet established
- Names to decide

**Format:**
```markdown
## Pending Details
- [Character]'s hometown - not yet named
- Magic system cost - keep vague until Chapter 8
- Antagonist's real motivation - reveal in Chapter 12
```

---

## Edits Needed Section

Track fixes for revision pass:

- Continuity errors caught too late
- Name changes that need global update
- Foreshadowing to add earlier
- Scenes to strengthen

**Format:**
```markdown
## Edits Needed
- Chapter 3: Character X says Y, but Chapter 7 contradicts this
- Need to add foreshadowing for [reveal] in earlier chapters
- Chapter 5 pacing drags - tighten in revision
```

---

## Chapter Completion Log

Track what each chapter establishes:

| Ch | Title | Words | Key Achievement |
|----|-------|-------|-----------------|
| 1 | [Title] | ~2,500 | Introduced protagonist, established setting |
| 2 | [Title] | ~2,200 | First meeting with antagonist, stakes raised |

**Key Achievement** examples:
- "First appearance of [character]"
- "Relationship milestone: [X and Y trust established]"
- "Major reveal: [information]"
- "World rule established: [rule]"
- "Emotional turning point: [shift]"

---

## Reference Files Section

Quick reference to project files:

```markdown
## Reference Files
- `[WorkingTitle]_style.md` - Voice, POV, forbidden phrases
- `[WorkingTitle]_characters.csv` - Character details
- `[WorkingTitle]_world.md` - Setting and rules
- `[WorkingTitle]_outline.md` - Story structure
```

Update if new files added (like detailed chapter outlines).
