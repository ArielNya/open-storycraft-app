# Pacing by Story Section

How prose style shifts across the story arc.

---

## Opening / Act I

### Characteristics
- Establish voice strongly
- More observation and world-grounding
- Internal monologue heavier
- Slower, wider lens
- Reader getting oriented

### Prose Style
- Longer sentences with room to breathe
- Description can be more expansive
- Character interiority establishes personality
- Set the baseline rhythm

### Purpose
The opening teaches readers how to read your book. The pace you set here becomes the baseline that later sections deviate from.

---

## Middle / Rising Action

### Characteristics
- Tighter focus
- More dialogue
- Internal monologue more urgent
- Pacing accelerates toward turning points

### Prose Style
- Sentences begin shortening
- Less room for wandering description
- Internal thought gets more reactive
- Scenes cut faster

### Purpose
Build momentum. Each scene should feel like it matters more than the last. The prose mirrors the tightening stakes.

---

## Climax

### Characteristics
- Fastest pacing
- Shortest sentences (but still complete)
- Minimal internal thought (action dominates)
- NO fragments - short complete sentences create urgency without breaking grammar

### Prose Style
- Short, punchy sentences
- Cut everything non-essential
- Present action beats only
- Reader should feel breathless

### Purpose
This is the release of built tension. Don't slow down for reflection. Let the action carry.

### Example
> The door gave way. She burst through. Three guards waited inside. She had no time to think. She fired once, twice. The third one ran.

---

## Resolution

### Characteristics
- Slows down deliberately
- Returns to observation
- Processing and reflection
- Echoes opening style (bookend effect)

### Prose Style
- Longer sentences return
- Room for emotional processing
- Sensory details ground the new reality
- Internal monologue allowed to breathe

### Purpose
Give the reader (and character) time to process what happened. The contrast with the climax's pace makes the resolution feel earned.

---

## Transitions

### Scene Breaks
- White space or symbol break
- Don't need transitional paragraphs
- Jump cuts are fine

### Time Skips
- Brief orientation line
- "Three days later" or "By morning"
- Don't over-explain

### Tonal Shifts
- Sentence length signals the shift
- First sentence of new section sets new pace
- Reader adjusts quickly
