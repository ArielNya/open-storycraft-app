# Horror — Genre Index

## Overview
Horror fiction exists to frighten. The core appeal is controlled fear — experiencing dread, revulsion, or terror in a safe context. Horror asks: "What if you encountered something you cannot understand, defeat, or escape?"

## Fear Mechanics
- **Dread**: Slow build. The reader knows something is wrong before the character does. Most effective, hardest to execute.
- **Shock**: Sudden, violent revelation. Jump scare equivalent in prose. Loses power with repetition.
- **Revulsion**: Physical disgust. Body horror, gore, visceral wrongness. Effective but audience-limiting.
- **Cosmic/Existential**: The horror of insignificance. Not a monster, but a realization about the universe.

## Subgenres

### Cosmic Horror
The universe is vast, indifferent, and contains things human minds cannot comprehend.

### Survival Horror
Characters trapped in a dangerous situation with limited resources. Escape or die.

### Psychological Horror
The horror comes from inside: unreliable perception, paranoia, gaslighting, mental breakdown.
**Detail file**: `horror-psychological.md`

### Gothic Horror
Decaying settings, family secrets, atmosphere of dread, romantic undertones.
**Detail file**: `horror-gothic.md`

### Body Horror
The human body as source of horror: transformation, infection, mutation, loss of bodily autonomy.
**Detail file**: `horror-body.md`

### Slasher
A killer hunts victims. High body count, identifiable antagonist, survival of the resourceful.
**Detail file**: `horror-slasher.md`

### Haunted House
A location is wrong. Characters investigate or are trapped. The setting is the antagonist.
**Detail file**: `horror-haunted.md`

### Folk Horror
Rural settings, old traditions, pagan or pre-Christian rituals, outsiders vs. community.
**Detail file**: `horror-folk.md`

### Apocalyptic Horror
The end of civilization as horror, not adventure. Focus on dread, not survival mechanics.
**Detail file**: `horror-apocalyptic.md`

### Quiet Horror
Subtle, atmospheric. The horror is understated — a growing wrongness, not a monster reveal.
**Detail file**: `horror-quiet.md`
