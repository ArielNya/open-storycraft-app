# Romance — Genre Index

## Overview
Romance fiction centers a love story as the primary plot. The genre's defining rule: **HEA (Happily Ever After) or HFN (Happy For Now) is mandatory.** Without it, it's not romance — it's a love story in another genre.

## Heat Level Spectrum
- **Sweet**: No explicit content. Closed door. Emotional intimacy only.
- **Warm**: Kissing, attraction described, fade to black at the bedroom door.
- **Hot**: Open door. Sex scenes present (2-4 per book), emotionally driven.
- **Scorching**: Frequent, detailed sex scenes. Erotica-adjacent.

These four labels describe a romance's overall temperature. For how physically
grounded the language on the page gets, which is a separate question, use the
door bands: **Closed Door (2)**, **Open Door (3-4)**, **Door Wide Open (5)**. Full
definitions and the craft failures of each band are in `heat-door-scale.md`. Set
the band at the premise stage and hold it; drifting up mid-book reads as a
different novel starting.

## Tropes as Structure
Romance tropes are not just flavor — they are **structural elements** that define the relationship arc. "Enemies to lovers" is not a tag; it is a specific trajectory from antagonism to vulnerability to trust.

## Subgenres

### Contemporary Romance
Set in the present day, real world. Career, family, and relationship dynamics.
**Detail file**: `romance-contemporary.md`

### Historical Romance
Set in a specific historical period. Period-accurate social constraints drive the tension.
**Detail file**: `romance-historical.md`

### Paranormal Romance
Supernatural beings (vampires, werewolves, fae) in romantic relationships. Often urban settings.
**Detail file**: `romance-paranormal.md`

### Fantasy Romance
Set in a fantasy world where the romance and the fantasy plot are co-equal drivers.
**Detail file**: `romance-fantasy.md`

### Sci-Fi Romance
Romance in a science fiction setting. Alien love interests, space travel, future societies.
**Detail file**: `romance-scifi.md`

### Romantic Suspense
Romance + thriller/mystery. The couple falls in love while solving a crime or escaping danger.
**Detail file**: `romance-suspense.md`

### Dark Romance
Morally complex or taboo relationships. Power dynamics, dubious consent (handled intentionally), anti-heroes.
**Detail file**: `romance-dark.md`

### Romantic Comedy (RomCom)
Humor-driven romance. Witty banter, comedic situations, light tone. The comedy IS the courtship.
**Detail file**: `romance-romcom.md`

### Slow Burn
The romance builds gradually over a long arc. Tension accumulates through proximity and restraint.
**Detail file**: `romance-slow-burn.md`

### Erotica
The sexual relationship is the plot rather than an event inside it. No HEA is
owed; transformation is owed instead. Door Wide Open by default.
**Detail file**: `romance-erotica.md`

---

## Cross-Indexed Modes

Not romance subgenres, and they do not inherit the mandatory HEA. Modes that can
sit on a romance spine or any other.

### Adult Light Novel
Anime/manga sensibility in prose. Archetype-forward cast, escalating intimacy as
structure, onomatopoeic intimate register.
**Detail file**: `lightnovel-adult.md`
