# Science Fiction — Genre Index

## Overview
Science fiction extrapolates from real or imagined science and technology to explore how humanity changes, adapts, or fails. The core appeal is ideas — "what if?" taken seriously.

## Subgenres

### Space Opera
Grand scale, interstellar travel, galactic politics, epic battles. Adventure in space.
**Detail file**: `scifi-space-opera.md`

### Hard Sci-Fi
Scientific accuracy drives the plot. Real physics, biology, or engineering as narrative engine.
**Detail file**: `scifi-hard.md`

### Cyberpunk
Near-future dystopia. Megacorporations, hacking, augmented bodies, neon-lit inequality.
**Detail file**: `scifi-cyberpunk.md`

### Military Sci-Fi
Future warfare. Soldiers, tactics, chain of command, alien enemies, advanced weaponry.
**Detail file**: `scifi-military.md`

### First Contact
Humanity meets alien intelligence for the first time. Communication, misunderstanding, wonder.
**Detail file**: `scifi-first-contact.md`

### Generation Ship
Enclosed society traveling to a distant star. Social breakdown, resource scarcity, forgotten origins.
**Detail file**: `scifi-generation-ship.md`

### Solarpunk
Optimistic future. Sustainable technology, community solutions, ecological harmony.
**Detail file**: `scifi-solarpunk.md`

### Biopunk
Biological technology: genetic engineering, organic machines, body modification as commodity.
**Detail file**: `scifi-biopunk.md`

### Time Travel
Characters move through time. Paradoxes, consequences, butterfly effects, temporal mechanics.
**Detail file**: `scifi-time-travel.md`

### Post-Apocalyptic
Civilization has collapsed. Survival, rebuilding, what remains of humanity.
**Detail file**: `scifi-post-apocalyptic.md`

### Colonization
Settling new worlds. Terraforming, cultural adaptation, conflict with indigenous life or other factions.
**Detail file**: `scifi-colonization.md`

### AI / Singularity
Artificial intelligence achieves or surpasses human-level cognition. What happens next.
**Detail file**: `scifi-ai.md`

---

## Cross-Indexed Modes

These are not sci-fi subgenres. They are modes that sit on top of any spine
above, including a sci-fi one.

### Adult Light Novel
Anime/manga sensibility in prose. Archetype-forward cast, escalating intimacy as
structure, onomatopoeic intimate register. Rides comfortably on a space opera,
colonization, or stranded-together sci-fi premise.
**Detail file**: `lightnovel-adult.md`
