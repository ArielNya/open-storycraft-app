# Thriller — Genre Index

## Overview
Thrillers generate sustained tension through high stakes, ticking clocks, and information asymmetry. Where mystery asks "whodunit?", thriller asks "what happens next?" The protagonist knows the danger and races against it.

## Thriller Mechanics
- **The Clock**: A deadline creates urgency. Without time pressure, a thriller becomes a mystery.
- **Information Asymmetry**: The reader and/or protagonist know something is wrong before it can be stopped.
- **Suspense vs Surprise**: Suspense = the reader knows the bomb is under the table. Surprise = the bomb goes off unexpectedly. Hitchcock taught us: suspense is almost always more effective.
- **Escalation**: Each obstacle must be worse than the last. The stakes ladder only goes up.
