# Literary Fiction — Genre Index

## Overview
Literary fiction prioritizes prose quality, thematic depth, and character interiority over plot velocity. The core appeal is insight — seeing the world or the human condition with fresh eyes through language that rewards close attention.

## Literary Fiction Mechanics
- **Prose style as product**: The sentences themselves are the point. A literary novel with generic prose has failed its genre.
- **Thematic depth over plot velocity**: Things don't need to "happen" at thriller pace. What happens internally is as significant as what happens externally.
- **Character interiority**: The reader lives inside the character's mind. Thought, perception, and memory are plot.
- **The sentence as unit of meaning**: Each sentence should do more than convey information — it should convey perspective, rhythm, or emotion.
- **Ambiguity as feature**: Literary fiction tolerates (and often requires) unresolved questions. The reader completes the meaning.

## Subgenres

### Contemporary Literary Fiction
Set in the present day. Character-driven, often domestic scope, thematic exploration of modern life.
**Detail file**: `literary-contemporary.md`

### Historical Literary Fiction
Set in a specific historical period. The era is not backdrop but subject — the past illuminates the present.
**Detail file**: `literary-historical.md`

### Magical Realism
The fantastical is presented as matter-of-fact within an otherwise realistic world. Magic without explanation or wonder.
**Detail file**: `literary-magical-realism.md`

### Autofiction
Fiction that draws heavily from the author's life. The boundary between author and protagonist is intentionally blurred.
**Detail file**: `literary-autofiction.md`

### Experimental Fiction
Form itself is the experiment. Unconventional structure, typography, narrative techniques, or genre blending.
