# Adventure — Genre Index

## Overview
Adventure fiction is about the journey — physical, dangerous, and transformative. The core appeal is vicarious experience: going somewhere exciting and surviving to tell the tale.

## Adventure Mechanics
- **Set-piece design**: Major action sequences that are memorable, distinct, and escalating. Each must top the last.
- **Stakes escalation**: Start personal, go larger. Start survivable, become existential.
- **The journey vs. the destination**: The travel itself must be interesting, not just filler between plot points.
- **Competence display**: The protagonist earns survival through skill, cleverness, and courage, not luck.

## Subgenres

### Action-Adventure
Non-stop movement. Fights, chases, explosions. Pace never drops below medium.

### Survival Adventure
Stranded, hostile environment. Limited resources. The setting is the antagonist.

### Heist
A team assembles to steal something. Planning, execution, complications. The plan goes wrong.

### Exploration
Discovering the unknown. Uncharted territory, lost civilizations, scientific expeditions.

### Quest
A goal to reach. Collect the artifacts, reach the mountain, deliver the message. The journey IS the story.

### Swashbuckler
Dashing heroes, swordplay, ships, and charisma. Style and flair as much as substance.

### Martial Arts
Combat as art form. Training, discipline, tournaments, rival schools, physical mastery.
