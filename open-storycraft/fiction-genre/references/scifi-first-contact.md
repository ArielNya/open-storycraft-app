# First Contact

**Definition**: Humanity encounters alien intelligence for the first time — the story is the meeting, the communication attempt, and the consequences.

## Mandatory Tropes
- **Alien Encounter**: Contact with a non-human intelligence.
- **Communication Challenge**: The difficulty of understanding a truly alien mind.
- **Paradigm Shift**: Humanity's understanding of its place changes forever.
- **Wonder and/or Fear**: The emotional response to the genuinely unknown.

## Optional Tropes
- **Linguistic Puzzle**: Cracking the alien language as the central challenge.
- **Peaceful Contact**: The aliens aren't hostile — the challenge is understanding.
- **Hostile Contact**: The aliens are a threat, and first contact is the opening of war.
- **Ambassador/Scientist Protagonist**: Someone chosen to bridge the gap.
- **Cultural Misunderstanding**: Actions that mean one thing to humans and another to aliens.
- **Technological Uplift**: Aliens sharing or withholding advanced technology.

## Reader Expectations
- **Promise**: The awe and terror of meeting something truly not like us.
- **Resolution**: Communication is achieved (or fails meaningfully). Humanity is changed.
- **Pacing**: Varies. Can be slow-burn puzzle or fast-paced thriller.
- **Tone**: Awe, wonder, tension. Often philosophical.
- **Satisfaction Source**: The moment of understanding (or the horror of realizing understanding is impossible).

## Pacing Conventions
- **Opening**: Establish humanity's status quo, then disrupt with contact.
- **Chapter Length**: 3,000-5,000 words.
- **Cliffhanger Frequency**: Moderate.
- **Act Structure**: 3-act. Discovery → Attempt → Resolution/Transformation.

## Comp Titles
1. **Arrival** (based on "Story of Your Life" by Ted Chiang) — Linguistic first contact.
2. **The Left Hand of Darkness** by Ursula K. Le Guin — Cultural first contact on an alien world.
3. **Blindsight** by Peter Watts — Hard sci-fi first contact, deeply unsettling.
4. **The Sparrow** by Mary Doria Russell — First contact as religious/philosophical crisis.
5. **Children of Time** by Adrian Tchaikovsky — First contact from both sides.

## Themes That Work
- **The limits of understanding**: Can truly alien minds ever understand each other?
- **Humanity's place in the cosmos**: Are we alone? Do we matter?
- **Communication as the hardest problem**: Language, culture, biology as barriers.
- **The Other**: What we project onto the unknown says more about us than them.

## Common Pitfalls
- **Aliens that are just humans in suits**: If the aliens think, feel, and communicate like people, they're not alien.
- **Easy communication**: Real first contact would be unimaginably difficult. Don't skip the hard part.
- **Ignoring the societal impact**: First contact changes everything — religion, politics, identity. Don't contain it to one lab.
