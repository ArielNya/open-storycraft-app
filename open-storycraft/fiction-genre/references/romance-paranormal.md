# Paranormal Romance

**Definition**: Romance where one or both protagonists are supernatural beings (vampires, werewolves, fae, shifters, demons, witches) and the paranormal element is inseparable from the central love story.

## Mandatory Tropes
- **HEA/HFN**: The couple must achieve a happily ever after or happy for now ending.
- **Fated or Impossible Bond**: The supernatural nature of the relationship creates unique stakes (mate bonds, immortality gaps, forbidden species pairings).
- **Paranormal World Rules**: The supernatural system must have consistent internal logic that shapes the romance's obstacles.
- **Power Imbalance**: One partner typically holds supernatural power the other does not, creating tension that must be resolved through trust.

## Optional Tropes
- **Fated Mates**: A destined bond that neither party can deny, though they may resist.
- **Forbidden Love**: Species, clans, or supernatural law prohibit the pairing.
- **Hidden World**: The supernatural community exists secretly alongside the human one.
- **Claiming/Marking**: A physical or magical act that seals the bond.
- **Alpha Protector**: A dominant supernatural being whose protective instincts border on obsessive.
- **Human Discovery**: A human partner learning the supernatural world exists.
- **Second Chance/Reincarnation**: Lovers reunited across lifetimes or after supernatural separation.
- **Pack/Coven Dynamics**: The couple's relationship disrupts or reshapes a supernatural community's power structure.

## Reader Expectations
- **Promise**: Love that transcends human limits, with passion amplified by supernatural stakes.
- **Resolution**: The bond is sealed, the threat neutralized, and the couple claims their place in the supernatural world together.
- **Pacing**: Fast. Action and attraction hit early and escalate together.
- **Tone**: Intense, sensual, often dark-edged. Ranges from fun and campy to brooding and gothic.
- **Satisfaction Source**: The primal, larger-than-life quality of the love and the sense that these two were always meant to find each other.

## Pacing Conventions
- **Opening**: Fast. Introduce the supernatural world and the attraction within the first chapter or two.
- **Chapter Length**: 3,000-5,000 words.
- **Cliffhanger Frequency**: Most chapters. Paranormal romance thrives on danger-plus-desire momentum.
- **Act Structure**: Three-act with series potential. Act 1 establishes the supernatural world and the charged first meeting. Act 2 deepens the bond while an external threat escalates. Act 3 resolves both the romantic and supernatural conflict. Many paranormal romances are part of multi-book series with interconnected couples.

## Comp Titles
1. **A Court of Thorns and Roses** by Ana J. Maas — Fae world, escalating power, and a romance that reshapes the protagonist.
2. **Dark Lover** by J.R. Ward — Vampire warrior romance with intense heat, world-building depth, and a found-family brotherhood.
3. **Moon Called** by Patricia Briggs — Shifter romance with a mechanic protagonist navigating werewolf pack politics.
4. **Lover Awakened** by J.R. Ward — A damaged vampire hero and the emotional depth that makes paranormal romance resonate beyond the tropes.
5. **Slave to Sensation** by Nalini Singh — Psy-Changeling world where psychic and shifter species collide in a forbidden bond.

## Themes That Work
- **Humanity in the Inhuman**: The supernatural partner rediscovering tenderness, vulnerability, or moral choice through love.
- **Acceptance of the Other**: Loving someone whose nature is genuinely alien or dangerous.
- **Power and Surrender**: Choosing to trust someone who could destroy you.
- **Immortality and Loss**: The weight of living forever and the human partner who makes eternity bearable.

## Common Pitfalls
- **Insta-Mate Without Earned Connection**: Fated bonds still require emotional development. "The bond says so" is not character chemistry.
- **World-Building Overload**: Elaborate supernatural hierarchies that bury the romance. The world serves the love story, not the reverse.
- **Passive Human Partner**: If one partner is supernatural, the human must still have agency, competence, and a reason the supernatural being respects them beyond attraction.
