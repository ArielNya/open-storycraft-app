# Fantasy — Genre Index

## Overview
Fantasy fiction features magic, supernatural elements, or imaginary worlds. The genre's core appeal is wonder — showing readers things that cannot exist and making them feel real.

## Subgenres

### Epic Fantasy
Vast scope, world-altering stakes, large casts. Good vs evil on a grand scale.
**Detail file**: `fantasy-epic.md`

### High Fantasy
Set entirely in a secondary world with its own rules, history, and geography.
**Detail file**: `fantasy-high.md`

### Low Fantasy
Magic intrudes into an otherwise mundane world, or exists at the margins.
**Detail file**: `fantasy-low.md`

### Dark Fantasy / Grimdark
Morally gray characters, brutal consequences, subverted heroic tropes.
**Detail file**: `fantasy-grimdark.md`

### Urban Fantasy
Magic in modern cities. Supernatural elements hidden within or alongside contemporary life.
**Detail file**: `fantasy-urban.md`

### Portal Fantasy / Isekai
Character transported from our world into a fantasy world.
**Detail file**: `fantasy-portal.md`

### LitRPG / Progression Fantasy
Game-like mechanics (stats, levels, skills) woven into narrative. Characters grow measurably stronger.
**Detail file**: `fantasy-litrpg.md`

### Cozy Fantasy
Low stakes, warm tone, found family. Comfort reading with fantastical elements.
**Detail file**: `fantasy-cozy.md`

### Romantic Fantasy
Fantasy world where the romance is co-equal to (or drives) the plot.
**Detail file**: `fantasy-romantic.md`

### Mythic Fantasy
Draws directly from real-world mythology, folklore, or legend.
**Detail file**: `fantasy-mythic.md`

### Gaslamp Fantasy
Victorian/Edwardian-era setting with magic alongside early industrial technology.
**Detail file**: `fantasy-gaslamp.md`

### Wuxia / Xianxia
Chinese-inspired martial arts cultivation fantasy. Characters ascend through power tiers via training and enlightenment.
**Detail file**: `fantasy-wuxia.md`

---

## Cross-Indexed Modes

These are not fantasy subgenres. They are modes that sit on top of any spine
above, including a fantasy one.

### Adult Light Novel
Anime/manga sensibility in prose. Archetype-forward cast, escalating intimacy as
structure, onomatopoeic intimate register. Commonly rides an isekai or secondary-
world fantasy premise.
**Detail file**: `lightnovel-adult.md`
