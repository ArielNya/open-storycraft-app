## Field Explanations

### Want vs Need
- **Want**: The external goal they're chasing (rescue the princess, win the competition, get revenge)
- **Need**: The internal requirement for fulfillment (learn to trust, accept loss, find self-worth)
- The gap between these creates story tension

### False Belief
What the protagonist believes at the start that's fundamentally wrong:
- "Strength means never needing help"
- "Love always leads to betrayal"
- "If I'm good enough, they'll accept me"

The story shatters this belief.

### Antagonist's Legitimate Motivation
Not "they're evil." Why do THEY think they're right?
- "The current system destroyed my family"
- "I'm protecting my people from a real threat"
- "They broke their promise to me first"

### The Cost
What victory actually requires sacrificing. Not vague ("everything"). Specific:
- The protagonist's comfortable identity
- A relationship they valued
- Their original goal itself
- A moral principle they held

### The Reversal
What assumed "truth" gets flipped:
- The mentor was the real threat
- The enemy was trying to warn them
- The treasure was a trap
- Their dead friend is alive but changed
