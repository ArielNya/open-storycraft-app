# Anti-Flat Synopsis Checklist

Before finalizing any synopsis, verify these elements are present. A flat synopsis leads to a flat story.

---

## Required Elements

### 1. Protagonist Wants Something WRONG or Incomplete
The initial goal is flawed. They're chasing the wrong thing, or the right thing for wrong reasons.

**Weak**: Protagonist wants to save the world
**Strong**: Protagonist wants to prove they're worthy by saving the world (the flaw is seeking external validation)

### 2. Antagonist Has Legitimate Motivation
From their perspective, they're justified. The reader should be able to see their point.

**Weak**: Wants power because evil
**Strong**: Wants power because the current rulers destroyed their homeland

### 3. At Least One Ally with Misaligned Agenda
Someone on the protagonist's side wants something different. This creates internal tension.

**Example**: Ally wants to defeat the tyrant but also wants revenge on a specific general the protagonist needs alive.

### 4. Stakes Are Personal Before Global
"Save the world" is abstract. Ground it in something specific the character would feel.

**Weak**: If they fail, the kingdom falls
**Strong**: If they fail, the kingdom falls AND the person they abandoned to start this quest dies believing they were never coming back

### 5. Transformation Requires Sacrifice
The change isn't free. What does growth cost them?

- A comfortable belief
- A valued relationship
- Their original goal
- Part of their identity

### 6. A False Belief That Shatters
Something the protagonist believes deeply that the story will prove wrong.

Not a fact they don't know (that's just information). A belief about how the world works that's fundamentally mistaken.

### 7. A Reversal That Recontextualizes
Something that makes the reader mentally replay earlier events with new understanding.

Not just "things got worse" but "everything we thought we understood was wrong."

---

## Quick Check

- [ ] Protagonist wants something WRONG or incomplete
- [ ] Antagonist has legitimate motivation
- [ ] At least one ally with misaligned agenda
- [ ] Stakes are personal before global
- [ ] Transformation requires sacrifice
- [ ] A false belief that shatters
- [ ] A reversal that recontextualizes
